Fit. Exact — the source category denotes the same thing as the response option. Broader / narrower — it covers more or less than the option. Contested — a defensible reading puts it under a different option; the alternative is named in the note. Decided — a judgement call that was put to the team and settled; the note records what was chosen and why. Awaiting data — cannot be resolved with the sources currently in hand. Unmapped — deliberately feeds nothing.
